Refunds are allowed within 30 days of purchase. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Shipping charges are not refunded. Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7 working days.

Delivery usually takes 5 working days. We deliver to all major cities across India. Express delivery is available for an extra fee and takes 2 days. Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next day.

To reset your password, click the Forgot Password link on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email. The link is valid for 24 hours. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder.

Customer support is available from 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. You can reach us by email or phone. We are closed on public holidays. Average response time for emails is 24 hours.

You can cancel an order before it is shipped. Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled but you can return it after delivery. To cancel, go to My Orders and click Cancel Order.
